## How It Works

### Merchant Configuration

The merchant configures:

- `payee address` — receives customer funds
- `Gnosis Safe address` — multisig used to review and approve fund pulls
- `approver email addresses` — notification list for pending Safe approvals

### Subscriber Authorization

At checkout, the subscriber selects the `Multisig` plan and signs a USDC `permit()` message.

- The signature authorizes spending without requiring an on-chain `approve()` tx from the subscriber
- The backend handles transaction submission
- The subscriber does not pay gas in this flow

### Merchant Approval Flow

After the subscriber signs:

- The merchant dashboard polls for updates every 10 seconds
- The PayPay Subscription Service emails the Safe approvers
- The approver opens the transaction in Safe
- The approver can inspect or simulate the transaction before approving it
- Once the Safe approval threshold is satisfied, the transaction can be submitted/executed

### Sequence

```text
subscriber -> checkout: click Subscribe
checkout -> wallet: sign USDC permit
backend -> paypay service: create pending pull transaction
paypay service -> merchant dashboard: status update (polled every 10s)
paypay service -> approvers: email notification
approver -> Safe: review / simulate / approve
Safe -> chain: submit when threshold is met
chain -> payee address: transfer USDC
```

## Long Narration (1m 19s)

Hi, I am YK from RUI Labs. Today I want to show a crypto subscription service we recently built.

We start in the merchant dashboard. In this example, the merchant is Acme. Acme has configured a plan called `Multisig`, priced at 1 USDC per minute for one billing cycle.

On the settings page, there are three important fields. The first is the payee address, which receives funds from customers. The second is the Gnosis Safe address, which is the multisig used to review and approve fund pulls from customers. The third is the list of approver email addresses. Whenever a Safe transaction needs approval, the service sends notifications to these approvers.

Next, we move to the checkout page. The wallet is already connected, and customer `0x26...` is subscribing to the `Multisig` plan. When the customer clicks `Subscribe`, they sign a USDC `permit()` message. Our backend handles transaction submission, so the customer does not need to pay gas.

After that, we return to the merchant dashboard. The dashboard polls for transaction updates every 10 seconds and shows the latest state.

Once the transaction is ready for merchant approval, the PayPay Subscription Service sends an email to the Safe approvers and redirects them to Gnosis Safe. In Safe, the approver can inspect the transaction details or run a simulation. If everything looks correct, they click `Approve`. Once the Safe threshold is met, the transaction can be submitted and the merchant receives the funds.

## Pros

- Gasless subscriber authorization when the token supports `permit()`
- Merchant-side approval is gated by Safe instead of a single operator key
- Safe simulation gives approvers a review step before execution
- Notification + dashboard polling makes operational status visible

## Cons

- This is not a fully autonomous recurring pull flow if Safe approval is required every cycle
- Multisig approval adds latency compared to a purely automated keeper flow
- Wallet signing UX still shows raw `permit()` data rather than explicit subscription terms
- Requires merchant signer availability to keep billing moving

## Limitations

- USDC `permit()` is EOA-oriented and does not work as a universal path for smart-account subscribers
- The doc does not define whether Safe approval is required on every billing cycle or only at specific checkpoints
- Permit expiry, nonce handling, and replay protection are not shown in the video
- The exact spender/executor model is not described here; this note only documents the demo flow
