## Recommended Video Set

| Video | Primary Audience | Length | Goal | Priority |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Executive overview | Balaji | 30-45s | Explain the value and the high-level flow | High |
| End-to-end product demo | Yash, mixed audience | 1-2 min | Show what the product does from setup to approval | High |
| Technical flow / architecture | Swaym | 2-4 min | Explain how the authorization and pull flow work | High |
| Subscriber-facing demo | Actual subscriber | 30-60s | Build trust and explain the user action simply | High |
| Risks / constraints / open questions | Balaji + Swaym | 1-2 min | Surface trade-offs and decision points | Medium |
| Failure / edge-case demo | Swaym | 1-2 min | Show operational and engineering maturity | Optional |

If only three videos can be prepared first:

1. Executive overview
2. Technical flow / architecture
3. Subscriber-facing demo

These three cover decision-maker, engineer, and end-user needs.

## Recording Order

Recommended recording order:

1. Subscriber-facing demo
2. Executive overview
3. End-to-end product demo
4. Technical flow / architecture
5. Risks / constraints
6. Failure / edge-case demo

Reason:

- the subscriber and executive versions force the clearest language
- once those are clear, the product and technical versions are easier to adapt
- the risk and edge-case versions can be recorded last based on remaining time

## Practical Next Step

Before the next outreach, prepare at least:

1. one 45s subscriber-friendly video
2. one 45s executive video
3. one 2-3 min technical video

That gives a usable package even if the final audience is still unknown.
